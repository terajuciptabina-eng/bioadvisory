Design principles

Built to show what is known — and what is not.

01

Evidence over confident guesses

Future analysis must disclose its intended image type, source, limitations and uncertainty. An image alone may not establish a diagnosis.

02

Different images need different models

Clinical photographs, microscopy, radiology and pathology images have different tasks, datasets, risks and validation requirements.

03

Human decision-making stays central

References and software flags support learning and research. Qualified clinicians interpret results in context and determine care.

04 / Research & safety

Clear scope. Measurable next steps.

A roadmap from a static prototype to a responsibly evaluated research platform.

What works today

Medical image intake previewBrowser-local image selection and preview; no upload, inference or diagnosis.
Disease reference starter setThree educational entries with links to public-health references.
Microbiology consistency checksDeterministic rules over synthetic fields with explanations and JSON export.
Responsive interfaceSingle-page static app; no backend or external runtime dependencies.

What must be built and validated

1. Define intended useChoose the initial image type and narrowly scoped research task before selecting a model.
2. Build a safe inference serviceConsent-aware data governance, access control, secure storage/processing and deletion policy.
3. Select and evaluate modelsDocument model/dataset provenance, external validation, calibration, subgroup performance and failure modes.
4. Clinical and regulatory reviewExpert adjudication, ethics approvals where applicable, and assessment of relevant local requirements.
Boundaries

Safety and data handling

  • Do not enter personal identifiers, patient records or confidential images.
  • Image preview uses a local browser object URL; this page does not send the image to a server.
  • There is no trained model, diagnostic inference, clinical validation, or treatment recommendation engine connected.
  • Educational disease content is a starter set and should be reviewed against current official guidance.
  • Do not rely on this prototype for emergency decisions, diagnosis or therapy.
