Disease reference library

A starter reference library with disease origin, typical clinical course/stages, and treatment principles. Search by disease or category. Content is educational and linked to public-health sources.

Important: this library is not a diagnosis and cannot determine a disease from an image. Stages and symptoms vary by person and disease. Treatment must be selected by a qualified clinician using current local guidance, test results, age, pregnancy status, allergies, comorbidities and resistance patterns. Do not self-medicate or delay urgent care.

Starter entries: tuberculosis, dengue and malaria. This is not a comprehensive disease database. Source links and guideline versions should be reviewed regularly.
